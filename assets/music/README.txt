PLACEHOLDER — YOUR SONG GOES HERE
=================================

1. Put your song in this folder and name it:  our-song.mp3
   (or use any name and change CONFIG.music.src in js/config.js)

2. That's it. A small "♪ Our song" button appears after she opens the
   envelope. Music never plays by itself on load, and her play/pause
   choice is remembered for next time.

Tips
- MP3 or M4A works on every phone. Keep it under ~5 MB so it loads fast.
- If the file is missing, the button simply hides itself.
- Don't want music at all? Set CONFIG.music.enabled = false.
